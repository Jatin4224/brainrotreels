## Your onboarding

Work through these in order. It takes about twenty minutes and it sets up everything else.

1. **[Introduce yourself](https://www.skool.com/ai-era/welcome-to-ai-era)** — tell us who you are and what you're building 👋

2. **[Mark the coaching calls on your calendar](https://www.skool.com/ai-era/classroom/b0b182a2?md=66b5650072d74e4e957db82702708f45)** — every call is recorded, but showing up live is where you get your questions answered 📅

3. **[Download the current version](https://www.skool.com/ai-era/classroom/b0b182a2?md=1e0fea3d3d634138b763381b5a40997b)** — I update this codebase constantly, so what you're holding may already be behind. Always start from the latest build 🔄

4. **[Post your idea for review](https://www.skool.com/ai-era/idea-locked-comment-it-below-and-ill-review-it)** — do this *before* you write anything. I'll record a custom Loom breaking down your idea and how to approach it 🎥

5. **Learn the prompting strategies** — this module covers how to prompt specifically against this architecture, so your AI builds inside the guardrails instead of around them 🧠

## Phase 2

Once you've shipped, Phase 2 unlocks. It covers how to actually launch — the licensing model, plus a few newer models working in 2026 that most people haven't caught onto yet 🔓
